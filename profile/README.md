## Medical Device Design Knowledge Graph

Hofstra University · Fred DeMatteis School of Engineering and Applied Science

We build open, queryable infrastructure for medical device design — linking FDA regulatory, safety and postmarket data that is otherwise published in separate, unconnected systems, and teaching engineering students to design with that evidence in hand.

### medical-device-kg

An RDF knowledge graph of United States FDA medical device data — roughly 17.7 million triples spanning premarket review, product classification, recognized consensus standards, recalls, adverse events, postmarket surveillance studies and establishment registrations. Published on the NSF Proto-OKN Fabric.

SPARQL endpoint: https://apps.okn.us/medical-device-kg/sparql

Registry entry: https://registry.okn.us/registry/kgs/medical-device-kg/

Persistent identifier: https://purl.org/okn/frink/kg/medical-device-kg

### bmedesign.org

An open-access medical device design education platform for biomedical engineering students. https://www.bmedesign.org

### Please note

Adverse event counts in these datasets reflect reporting behavior and market exposure, not device failure rates. Nothing published here is regulatory or medical advice, and the U.S. Food and Drug Administration does not endorse this work.
